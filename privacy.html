Jovér Bot — Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Jovér Bot handles information when used on Discord servers.

Information We Collect

Jovér Bot may process information provided through Discord interactions, such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, messages, commands, and other information required for the bot's features.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to provide, maintain, and improve the bot's features and functionality.

Data Storage

Jovér Bot does not intentionally collect or store personal information beyond what is necessary for its features and operation.

Third-Party Services

Jovér Bot operates through Discord and may rely on Discord's services. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Data Requests

If you have questions about data processed by Jovér Bot, you may contact the Jovér Bot team through the official support server.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary.
